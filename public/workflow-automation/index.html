The hard part isn’t repeating clicks.

It lives between tools.

You switch context, stitch information together, and remember what happened where.

The inputs keep changing.

Formats shift, fields move, and exceptions pile up faster than a macro can keep up.

The judgment is undocumented.

Critical checks live in one operator’s head, not in a process someone else can run.
